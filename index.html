About Me

Hi! I'm Lami Langa, a Data Scientist and Data Analyst based in Pretoria, South Africa.

I build models that answer a business question, and I build the thing around them that makes the answer usable — the pipeline that feeds it, the features underneath it, and the interface that puts it in front of someone who doesn't write code.

Most of my work sits in the financial and consumer space: classification, scoring and forecasting on transactional data. I'm drawn to the messy part, where the raw record is inconsistent and the job is to make it mean something.

I care about honest results. Proper validation, assumptions written down, and uncertainty explained plainly rather than a number that looks confident and isn't.
